1. Understanding the Venue: Expo Centre Lahore Infrastructure

Located in Johar Town, Expo Centre Lahore is Pakistan's premier trade exposition facility. Covering thousands of square meters across Hall 1, Hall 2, Hall 3, the Central Foyer, and the Outdoor Exhibition Area, it hosts major industrial fairs including the Pakistan Auto Show (PAPS), ITCN Asia, Build Pakistan, and multinational medical congresses.

Each hall features polished concrete industrial floors, utility service trenches beneath the aisles, and an 8.0-meter clear ceiling height. However, navigating hall logistics requires precise coordination with the venue's technical directorate. Power hookups must be formally requisitioned with single-line diagrams, fire safety certifications must accompany all paint applications, and structural hanging points are strictly limited to rated ceiling trusses.

4. Essential Expo Centre Lahore Rules & Engineering SOPs

To prevent on-site fines, hall clearance delays, or safety shutdowns by venue marshals, every exhibitor must adhere strictly to these engineering parameters:

A. Height Restrictions & Setbacks

Standard booths: 2.44m (8ft). Custom wooden stalls: Up to 4.5m with boundary setbacks. Double-decker structures: Up to 5.5m - 6.0m, requiring a certified structural engineer's stability certificate submitted at least 14 days before move-in.

B. Electrical Load Balancing & Circuit Breakers

Exhibitors must never plug heavy machinery, air-warmers, or high-draw lighting into standard domestic extension cords. Pro Event Solutions installs industrial 3-phase Distribution Boards (DBs) with calibrated MCBs and heavy-duty rubber cable protectors across aisles.

C. Fire Safety & Non-Flammable Materials

All fabric draperies must be treated with certified fire-retardant sprays. Enclosed meeting rooms exceeding 15 square meters require mandatory smoke detector installation and an accessible fire extinguisher.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How much does a custom exhibition stall cost at Expo Centre Lahore? +

For custom wooden stalls at Expo Centre Lahore, prices typically range between PKR 1,200 to PKR 2,200 per square foot depending on materials (paint finishes, laminate flooring, acrylic 3D logos, and LED backlighting). Double-decker booths range between PKR 2,800 to PKR 4,500 per sq. ft. because of structural steel load testing.

What is the maximum permitted stall height at Expo Centre Lahore? +

Standard modular shell scheme booths are capped at 2.44 meters (8 feet). Custom single-story raw-space wooden stalls are typically approved up to 4.5 meters, while double-decker pavilions can reach up to 5.5 to 6.0 meters in Halls 1, 2, and 3, subject to official structural engineer certification and venue NOC.

How far in advance should we confirm stall fabrication? +

We recommend confirming your stall 3 to 4 weeks prior to the official move-in date. This allows 7 days for 3D architectural CAD modeling and structural approval, 10-14 days for precision workshop pre-fabrication at our Rajgarh facility, and smooth 48-hour on-site installation.
